For [Scott+Scott](https://scott-scott.com/), an international law firm, I was responsible for the technical stewardship of their core web platform. The objective was to modernize a robust WordPress ecosystem by improving user experience through custom component development and drastically improving page performance to support their digital strategy.

![Scott+Scott](/projects/scott-scott/image.png)

## Key Contributions & Technical Achievements

- **Frontend Component Engineering:** Designed, built, and integrated bespoke interactive UX components natively within the WordPress environment. This elevated the user interface and made critical legal information significantly more accessible and engaging for prospective clients.
- **Performance Optimization (Core Web Vitals):** Spearheaded a deep-dive performance audit resulting in strategic optimizations across the entire site. Through asset minification, advanced caching architectures, and optimized image delivery workflows, I drastically reduced page load times and improved Lighthouse tracking scores.
- **Enterprise Maintenance & Security:** Managed the ongoing maintenance lifecycle of the WordPress site. This included ensuring robust backend security patching, database optimization, and ensuring zero-downtime reliability for high-traffic announcements. 

## Business Impact

The holistic optimizations transformed the website into a much faster, more agile, and highly resilient platform. The improved Core Web Vitals directly supported organic SEO initiatives, while the responsive custom components provided a seamless and premium experience for clients seeking sophisticated legal counsel.
