During my tenure at [FuelBuddy](https://fuelbuddy.in/), I played a core engineering role in developing and scaling their primary **Franchise Application**. This platform serves as the central nervous system for franchise operators, enabling them to manage complex local hubs, oversee inventory, and orchestrate their localized delivery fleets.

## Core Technical Contributions

- **Real-Time Fleet Tracking:** Architected and deployed a highly requested "Truck Tracking" module. By synchronizing geolocation signals with an interactive map interface, I provided operators with live, granular visibility into their fleet. This feature fundamentally changed how dispatching decisions were made.
- **API Performance Optimization:** Conducted deep performance audits on the application's data layer. By restructuring API payloads, implementing smart caching, and minimizing expensive database queries, I drastically reduced response latencies. This ensured the app remained highly performant even in areas with poor cellular reception.
- **UX Restructuring & Workflow Design:** Spearheaded a targeted initiative to improve the user experience for non-technical franchise staff. I redesigned complex, multi-step tasks (like inventory reconciliation and driver assignment) into frictionless, intuitive user journeys, resulting in a significantly lower operational error rate.

## Business Impact

The delivery of the fleet tracking feature immediately improved logistical predictability and reduced truck idle times. Furthermore, the combination of a snappier, optimized backend and a deeply refined frontend UX directly accelerated onboarding times for new franchise partners and heavily reduced support tickets related to app usability.
