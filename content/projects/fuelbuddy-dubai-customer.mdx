To support FuelBuddy's strategic expansion into the highly lucrative B2B ecosystem of the UAE, the business required a dedicated, enterprise-grade web application. Unlike our consumer-facing mobile solutions, this portal needed to handle complex corporate workflows and bulk management. I was entrusted to help architect and build the frontend of this critical web platform entirely from scratch.

## Technical Scope & Contributions

- **Greenfield Web Architecture:** Bootstrapped the frontend foundation from a blank slate. By establishing the core component architecture, routing strategies, and global styling systems, I laid the groundwork for a highly scalable and maintainable enterprise web application.
- **B2B Workflow Engineering:** Engineered sophisticated end-to-end user journeys tailored exclusively for corporate logistics. This involved designing interfaces for complex form states, dynamic bulk-ordering workflows, and the presentation of dense dashboard data crucial for B2B fleet managers.
- **End-to-End Feature Ownership:** Acted as a central pillar throughout the development lifecycle. I worked autonomously to bridge the gap between design mockups and business logic, tying meticulously crafted frontend layouts to the backend APIs, delivering fully complete features straight to production.

## Business Impact

Delivering this web portal from the ground up successfully anchored FuelBuddy’s digital footprint within the corporate Dubai market. The resulting platform provided enterprise clients with a reliable, desktop-optimized web experience, drastically reducing friction in large-scale B2B fuel orders and securely accelerating the company's regional B2B revenue pipeline.
