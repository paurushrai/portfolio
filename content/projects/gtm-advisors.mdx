For [GTM Advisors](https://gtmadvisors.com/), the goal was to build a digital presence that perfectly mirrored their high-impact consulting services. Working from a blank canvas, I architected and engineered a modern, highly performant marketing platform utilizing the **Jamstack** philosophy.

![GTM Advisors](/projects/gtm-advisors/image.png)

## Technical Architecture & Delivery

- **Astro.js Static Generation:** Chose Astro for its unparalleled ability to ship zero JavaScript by default. This led to lightning-fast page load times, scoring exceptional marks on Google Lighthouse, and providing a fundamental advantage for Search Engine Optimization (SEO).
- **Headless CMS Integration (Directus):** Completely decoupled the frontend from the data layer by implementing Directus CMS. I structured the database schemas and exposed RESTful APIs, empowering the marketing team with full autonomy over content without risking code integrity.
- **Tailored UI/UX Engineering:** Translated design mockups into a responsive, pixel-perfect frontend. The component-driven architecture allows for highly scalable and maintainable code as the site grows.

## Business Impact

By transitioning to a modern Jamstack ecosystem, GTM Advisors drastically reduced their hosting footprint while unlocking enterprise-grade speed. Content updates are now frictionless, and the technical SEO foundation actively drives increased organic discovery and lead generation.
