When iOS ecosystem restrictions began limiting the functionality of FuelBuddy's legacy Progressive Web App (PWA), the business faced a critical hurdle in retaining Apple users. Under the guidance of our Tech Lead, I was part of the core engineering task force responsible for migrating the entire consumer platform to a robust, cross-platform **React Native** application.

## Technical Scope & Contributions

- **Strategic Mobile Migration:** Executed a massive rewrite from a PWA to a compiled React Native application. I was heavily responsible for engineering complex mobile interfaces, ensuring that the new application achieved smooth, native-level performance across both iOS and Android environments.
- **Autonomous Feature Delivery:** While my primary mandate was driving the mobile frontend alongside a team of developers, I actively operated across the stack to prevent delivery bottlenecks. Whenever a new feature required custom data structures, I independently authored and integrated the necessary backend API endpoints.
- **Collaborative Engineering:** Thrived in a highly synchronized, cross-functional environment. I collaborated intimately with other developers and product owners to rapidly ship end-to-end features, translating high-level business logic into a flawless mobile experience. 

## Business Impact

The decisive pivot to React Native successfully neutralized the platform-level threat to iOS users, instantly recovering and expanding FuelBuddy's mobile market share. By functioning as a versatile, full-stack contributor, I helped the team dramatically accelerate the release cadence, getting a unified, highly polished native app straight into the hands of end consumers.
