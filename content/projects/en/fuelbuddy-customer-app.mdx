FuelBuddy's customer app started as a Progressive Web App. That worked on Android, but on iOS it faced platform limitations: WebKit engine constraints, unreliable install-to-home-screen, limited storage, and missing push notification capabilities. For FuelBuddy, that meant the PWA could not reliably serve iOS users, and a native app was necessary to provide a stable mobile experience. I was part of the engineering team that rewrote the app in React Native.

## What I worked on

- **PWA to React Native:** Rewrote the app as a compiled React Native application, hitting 100% feature parity across 14 user flows in 8 weeks and ensuring a consistent native feel on both iOS and Android.
- **Working across the stack:** Focused on the mobile frontend while building necessary CRUD APIs directly on Hasura GraphQL engine so feature development was never blocked on backend dependencies.
- **Shipping with the team:** Collaborated closely with frontend engineers and product managers to translate requirements into tested features and keep production release cycles on schedule.

## Why it mattered

Migrating to React Native established a reliable, single codebase across both iOS and Android. Shipping 14 user flows with full parity in 8 weeks allowed FuelBuddy to serve its entire mobile user base without maintaining separate platform implementations.
