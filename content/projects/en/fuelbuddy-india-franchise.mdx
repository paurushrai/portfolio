At [FuelBuddy](https://fuelbuddy.in/), I was a core engineer on the Franchise Application, which franchise operators use to manage regional hubs, inventory, and delivery fleets.

## What I built

- **Live truck tracking:** Built an interactive Google Maps module displaying fleet locations on a polling interval, giving operators continuous visibility into truck movements and dispatch statuses.
- **GraphQL APIs and caching:** Built data APIs using Hasura GraphQL engine through urql, with a normalized client-side cache and automatic token refresh on expiry.
- **Workflow simplification:** Redesigned multi-step operational tasks, inventory reconciliation and driver assignment among them, into streamlined interfaces that reduced operational errors.

## Why it mattered

Fleet tracking made dispatch logistics more predictable and reduced truck idle time. Streamlined assignment and inventory workflows reduced operational errors across franchise hubs.

## What "Live" Actually Means Here

The truck map updates on a polling interval, not a GraphQL subscription, even though the same client is wired up for subscriptions elsewhere in the app (`graphql-ws` is configured and used, just not for this particular map).

## Assignment and Inventory, in More Detail

Driver assignment covers single, bulk, reassignment, and unassignment flows, not just a single "assign" action. Inventory tracking follows stock balances for fuel bowsers (tanker trucks) specifically, with dedicated stock-balance queries and log tables, a real operational data model, not a generic parts inventory.

## Data Model & Scale

- urql's normalized cache plus a Firebase-token auth layer that refreshes automatically on 401/expired-token responses, so an expired session doesn't interrupt a dispatcher mid-task.
- Playwright covers login and order management end to end, the two flows every other screen in the app depends on.

## Stack

[Vue](https://vuejs.org/) 3, [Vite](https://vite.dev/), [Pinia](https://pinia.vuejs.org/), [urql](https://commerce.nearform.com/open-source/urql/) against [Hasura](https://hasura.io/), [Firebase](https://firebase.google.com/) auth, Google Maps, [Playwright](https://playwright.dev/) for tests.
