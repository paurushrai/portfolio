For [Scott+Scott](https://scott-scott.com/), an international law firm, I looked after their main WordPress site. The goal was to modernize a large existing site — better UX through custom components, and meaningfully faster pages.

![Scott+Scott](/projects/scott-scott/image.png)

## What I did

- **Custom components:** Built interactive frontend components inside WordPress that made the site's legal content easier to read and navigate for prospective clients.
- **Core Web Vitals:** I flagged the performance problems, made the case for fixing them, and drove the work — asset minification, caching, and better image delivery — bringing down load times and improving the site's Lighthouse scores.
- **Maintenance and security:** Owned the ongoing upkeep — security patching, database optimization, and coordinating with the firm to keep the site reliable through their high-traffic press announcements.

## Why it mattered

The site ended up faster and more stable. The Core Web Vitals improvements helped its search ranking, and the custom components gave prospective clients a cleaner experience.
