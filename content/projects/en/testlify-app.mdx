[Testlify](https://testlify.com/) is a skills assessment and interviewing platform used by organizations to evaluate candidates. I built the frontend architecture for Testlify's multi-modal AI interview modules, collaborating with AI engineers and backend teams to define real-time interaction flows.

## What I built

- **Multi-modal AI interview UI:** Developed real-time interfaces supporting text chat, live voice interaction, and video assessment modes.
- **Browser media capture:** Implemented browser MediaStream APIs for audio and video capture, device permission handling, live audio waveform visualization, and low-latency interaction feedback.
- **State and connection management:** Engineered client-side state handling to manage question sequencing, network reconnection scenarios, and AI processing states smoothly during live assessments.

## Why it mattered

Delivering a responsive, dependable interview interface allowed automated AI evaluations to scale across enterprise candidate pipelines while reducing administrative overhead for hiring teams.
