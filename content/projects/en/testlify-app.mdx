[Testlify](https://testlify.com/) is an advanced, AI-powered skills assessment and interviewing platform designed to revolutionize how enterprise companies evaluate applicant talent. As a frontend engineer on this platform, I was tasked with building the highly interactive, real-time user interfaces that facilitate Testlify's flagship AI interview modules.

## Technical Scope & Contributions

- **Immersive AI Interfaces (Voice, Video, & Chat):** Spearheaded the frontend implementation of multi-modal AI interviews. I built fluid, distraction-free micro-applications that allowed candidates to interact flawlessly with AI agents via text chat, live microphone inputs, and active video streaming.
- **Hardware API & Stream Management:** Working effectively on the client side, I managed the intricate browser APIs required for real-time media ingestion. This included elegantly handling microphone and camera permissions, rendering live audio visualizations to assure candidates they were being heard, and optimizing client-side performance to maintain a conversational, human-like AI cadence.
- **Resilient Assessment State Machines:** Constructed robust frontend state architectures to handle the unpredictable nature of live, generative interviews. I ensured the application gracefully managed network interruptions, handled asynchronous loading states while the AI generated follow-up questions, and processed complex assessment logic without ever halting the candidate's momentum.

## Business Impact

By delivering an incredibly polished and intuitive frontend for these AI capabilities, the platform drastically elevated the overall quality of mass talent assessments. These automated, interactive workflows successfully reduced the manual administrative effort required by human interviewers by over **40%**. The resulting operational efficiency and premier candidate experience played a direct, measurable role in contributing to a **20–30% growth in platform revenue**.
