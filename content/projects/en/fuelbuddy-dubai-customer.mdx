FuelBuddy's move into the UAE B2B market needed a dedicated web app — corporate customers there work differently from the consumer mobile users, with bulk orders and fleet management rather than one-off purchases. I helped build this portal from a blank repo — frontend and the CRUD APIs behind it.

## What I built

- **The foundation:** Set up the frontend from scratch — component structure, routing, and the shared styling system — so the app had a solid base to grow on.
- **B2B workflows:** Built the screens corporate customers actually use: multi-step ordering forms, bulk-order flows, and the dense dashboards fleet managers rely on.
- **Feature ownership:** Worked through features end to end — building the CRUD APIs on Hasura's GraphQL engine, then wiring the designs and business rules to them and getting the whole thing into production.

## Why it mattered

Shipping the portal gave FuelBuddy a proper web presence for corporate customers in Dubai and made large B2B fuel orders easier to place, which supported the company's push into the region.
