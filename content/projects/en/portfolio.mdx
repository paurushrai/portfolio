This portfolio is built with Next.js 14 App Router and maintained as an open-source product. The codebase is publicly accessible on GitHub with verified Lighthouse scores of 99/100 on desktop and 97/100 on mobile, along with 100/100 on Accessibility and SEO.

![Personal Portfolio - paurushrai.in](/projects/portfolio/portfolio.gif)

## What is in it

- **12-language localization:** Full support for English, German, Spanish, French, Hindi, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, Russian, Turkish, and Chinese. Driven by a type-safe language context and translation dictionary, updating the HTML lang tag and canonical URLs dynamically for international SEO.
- **Optimized canvas particle system:** Interactive particle background drawn on HTML5 canvas. Cached coordinate calculations and lazy loading ensure 60fps animations without impacting initial page paint.
- **First-party analytics proxy:** Custom serverless proxy routing for privacy-respecting analytics (Umami and Tag Manager), preventing data loss from browser ad-blockers while preserving visitor privacy.
- **Technical SEO and accessibility:** Structured JSON-LD schema markup for Person and Projects, per-locale canonical and alternate links, automated OpenGraph cards, skip-to-content navigation, and full keyboard accessibility.
- **Type-safe Contentlayer pipeline:** Projects and blog posts are authored in localized MDX files, validated at build time, and statically rendered for fast performance.

## Why it mattered

The site serves as a live, verifiable demonstration of engineering standards: strict TypeScript typing, comprehensive multi-language support, web performance optimization, and accessible UI design.
