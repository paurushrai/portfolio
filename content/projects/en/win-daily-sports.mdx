For [Win Daily Sports](https://windailysports.com/), I built sports-betting projection models on top of an automated pipeline that pulls in, processes, and analyzes large volumes of sports data.

![Win Daily Sports Dashboard](/projects/win-daily-sports/image.png)

## What I built

- **ETL pipelines:** Wrote scraping scripts to pull real-time and historical sports data from various sites and APIs.
- **Projection models:** Built the models that analyze player and team stats to surface high-value betting opportunities.
- **Automation:** Automated the whole flow from extraction through model runs, so the data stayed fresh with little manual work.
- **Google Sheets delivery:** Worked with the analysts to understand how they actually worked, then wired the model output into Google Sheets via the API and Apps Script — meeting them in the tool they already lived in rather than making them learn a new one.

## Why it mattered

Automating collection and modeling cut the time analysts spent getting to insight, and the projections gave the team a more consistent, data-backed basis for their predictions.
