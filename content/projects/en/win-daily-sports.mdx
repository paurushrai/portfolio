For [Win Daily Sports](https://windailysports.com/), I engineered sports projection models and automated data pipelines that extract, process, and analyze high-volume sports statistics across leagues.

![Win Daily Sports Dashboard](/projects/win-daily-sports/image.png)

## What I built

- **Automated ETL pipelines:** Built extraction and transformation scripts to ingest live and historical sports data from external feeds and statistical APIs.
- **Projection models:** Developed calculation models analyzing player performance metrics and team trends to identify statistical advantages.
- **Automation workflows:** Automated the end-to-end data pipeline from raw ingestion to model execution, keeping datasets updated with minimal manual intervention.
- **Google Sheets integration:** Connected model outputs directly to Google Sheets via the Google Sheets API and Apps Script, delivering live analysis directly into the tools the analyst team used daily.

## Why it mattered

Automating data collection and model execution eliminated manual data entry for analysts and provided a consistent, data-backed foundation for daily sports analysis.
