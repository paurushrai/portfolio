FuelBuddy's UAE driver app is what drivers use to run a fuel-delivery shift: from a pre-shift vehicle check through proof-of-delivery to dispatch paperwork. I built this app's frontend, covering the full shift workflow rather than just order logging.

## What I built

- **Shift and vehicle workflows:** Built asset selection, pre-shift health checks, and odometer logging as a connected sequence rather than standalone forms.
- **Meter-based proof of delivery:** Built before/after totalizer (fuel meter) reading capture, the actual mechanism that proves how much fuel was delivered, plus signature capture and delivery-photo upload.
- **Dispatch documents:** Built Challan (dispatch/delivery document) generation as part of the delivery flow.
- **Location and identification:** Integrated live GPS, map display, and QR scanning for asset identification.

## Why it mattered

Meter readings and signed delivery documentation, captured directly in the driver's workflow, gave FuelBuddy a verifiable record of every delivery without a separate paper process.

## What Proof of Delivery Actually Means Here

The core of this app isn't order browsing, it's compliance: a health check before the shift starts, an odometer reading, a totalizer reading before dispensing and another after, a signature, and a delivery photo. Together those readings are the evidence that a delivery happened and how much fuel moved, not just a status flag flipped in a database.

## Session State and Reliability

Pinia state persists across reloads through `pinia-plugin-persistedstate`, so a shift in progress survives a refresh or an app restart, useful given drivers working in the field on variable connectivity. Sentry is wired in for error tracking in production. OTP handles login rather than a password, and QR scanning identifies assets without manual entry.

## Data Model & Scale

- GraphQL runs through `graphql-request` against Hasura, the same pattern used across FuelBuddy's other UAE apps.
- Delivery photos are compressed client-side before upload, keeping proof-of-delivery evidence lightweight on a driver's mobile connection rather than uploading raw camera output.
- Distance and routing calculations run through Turf.js on top of the live GPS feed, not just a raw coordinate pair.

## Stack

[Vue](https://vuejs.org/) 3, [Vite](https://vite.dev/), [Pinia](https://pinia.vuejs.org/) with persisted state, [graphql-request](https://github.com/jasonkuhrt/graphql-request) against [Hasura](https://hasura.io/), [Mapbox](https://www.mapbox.com/) and Google Maps, [Sentry](https://sentry.io/) for error tracking, [jsQR](https://github.com/cozmo/jsQR) for QR scanning, [vue3-signature](https://www.npmjs.com/package/vue3-signature) for delivery sign-off.
