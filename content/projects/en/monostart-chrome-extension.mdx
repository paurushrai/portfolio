You open a new tab dozens of times a day and get a blank screen. MonoStart replaces it with a dashboard you set up yourself — your links, tasks, notes, and reminders, arranged how you want them. I built it as a Manifest V3 Chrome extension that runs entirely on your device.

![MonoStart — Customizable New-Tab Chrome Extension](/projects/monostart-chrome-extension/cover.png)

## What I built

- **A dashboard you arrange:** A drag-and-drop grid where every widget — links, grouped folders, notes, to-dos, timers, reminders, images, embeds, and search — can be placed and resized to fit how you work.
- **Everything one glance away:** Quick links and folders instead of digging through bookmarks; notes, tasks, and reminders on the page you already open all day.
- **Make it yours:** 12 color themes, a custom-color picker, light/dark, and color, gradient, or image wallpapers.
- **Timers and reminders:** Built in, so you don't reach for a separate app to stay on track.
- **Private by default:** Everything lives on your device — no account, no servers, no tracking — and the page opens instantly.

## Why it mattered

The new-tab page is the most-opened screen in the browser and usually does nothing. MonoStart turns it into a place that's actually useful, pulling links, tasks, notes, and reminders into one spot instead of scattering them across bookmarks, sticky notes, and other apps. It's a personal project and open source, so the code is there to read.
