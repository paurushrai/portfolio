Beyond order-taking apps, FuelBuddy's UAE operations needed a tool for managing customer and partner accounts and approving financial corrections. I built this admin panel's frontend.

## What I built

- **Account administration:** Built customer and partner account management, including linked physical assets, credit, and per-customer configuration.
- **Approval workflows:** Built approval flows for discounts, order cancellations, invoice-item corrections, task-value corrections, and purchase-order changes, each its own reviewed action rather than a direct edit.
- **Device calibration:** Built a calibration interface for physical metering devices tied to customer accounts.

## Why it mattered

Routing financial corrections and account changes through explicit approval steps gave FuelBuddy an auditable process for changes that directly affect billing, instead of ad hoc edits.

## Not Generic CRUD

The device-calibration feature is the clearest sign this isn't a generic admin-CRUD tool: it manages calibration parameters on a physical metering/dispensing device attached to a customer account, a real hardware-adjacent operation, not just editing a database row. The approval flows (discount, cancellation, invoice correction, task-value correction, purchase-order changes) exist as their own reviewed steps rather than direct edits, on purpose, for anything that touches billing.

## Data Model & Scale

- Exports to CSV and Excel are built in for financial and account reporting, not just on-screen tables.
- No automated tests were found in this repo; stated plainly rather than left unmentioned.

## Stack

[Vue](https://vuejs.org/) 3, [Vite](https://vite.dev/), [Pinia](https://pinia.vuejs.org/), [graphql-request](https://github.com/jasonkuhrt/graphql-request) with GraphQL Code Generator against [Hasura](https://hasura.io/), [Firebase](https://firebase.google.com/).
