[MetaNotes](https://app.metanotes.ai/) is an AI meeting assistant that joins Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, and Zoom calls to generate transcripts, action items, and conversation summaries. It is deployed as a public product and internally replaced a third-party transcription tool. I architected and built the frontend end to end.

![MetaNotes](/projects/metanotes/metanotes.gif)

## What I built

- **Transcript interface:** Built a virtualized transcript viewer that efficiently renders speaker-separated text, search highlights, and timestamps for long, multi-hour meetings.
- **Meeting intelligence dashboard:** Developed interactive views for one-click AI summaries, action item tracking, sentiment indicators, and topic tags.
- **Frontend architecture:** Served as the lead frontend engineer from initial concept to production, establishing state management, component architecture, and real-time event handling with backend AI pipelines.

## Why it mattered

MetaNotes launched as a public SaaS product and, when adopted internally at Testlify, eliminated **$24,000 in annual operational costs** previously paid to external transcription vendors.
