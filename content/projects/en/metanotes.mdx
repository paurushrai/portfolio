[MetaNotes](https://app.metanotes.ai/) is a powerful, AI-driven meeting assistant capable of joining Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, and Zoom calls to generate real-time transcripts, immediate summaries, and advanced conversational analytics. Tasked with replacing a costly third-party vendor with a superior internal product, I single-handedly architected and developed the entire frontend ecosystem for this ambitious platform.

![MetaNotes](/projects/metanotes/image.png)

## Technical Scope & Contributions

- **Real-Time Data Streaming:** Engineered the frontend to seamlessly ingest and render live data streams, elegantly displaying highly accurate rolling transcripts as conversations unfolded. I heavily optimized the DOM updates to ensure zero browser latency or layout shifting during active, hour-long meetings.
- **Complex UI/UX Architecture:** Designed and built a highly intuitive, dense-data dashboard. I integrated complex, modular interfaces for "One-click" AI-generated summaries, sentiment analytics, and dynamic "Smart Topic" tagging, allowing users to effortlessly parse and extract value from massive blocks of transcribed text.
- **Single-Handed Frontend Ownership:** Operated as the sole frontend engineer from concept to production. I established the foundational repository architecture, enforced strict state management, and flawlessly bridged the gap between the complex backend AI orchestration and a polished, consumer-grade user experience.

## Business Impact

By successfully engineering and deploying MetaNotes internally, the organization completely eliminated its reliance on expensive third-party AI transcription services, securing **$24,000 in annual recurring cost savings**. Furthermore, the blazing-fast, purpose-built frontend successfully automated the administrative burden of manual note-taking, delivering immediate, actionable insights and massive productivity boosts across professionals, students, and enterprise teams alike.
