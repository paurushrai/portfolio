At Testlify, engineering time tracking relied on an expensive third-party application that was disconnected from daily Jira workflows. I led the frontend development of Timesheets by AppForest, an integrated Jira application for time tracking, team capacity planning, and project cost allocation. The product is published on the Atlassian Marketplace and deployed internally across the company.

![Timesheets by AppForest - Overview](/projects/timesheets-appforest/timesheet.gif)

## What I built

- **Atlassian Marketplace integration:** Built against Jira REST and Connect APIs, adhering to enterprise security guidelines and scoped OAuth permissions.
- **Embedded Jira interface:** Designed a responsive interface embedded inside Jira issue panels, offering one-click time logging, calendar views, and daily/weekly timesheet submissions without context switching.
- **Reporting and analytics:** Developed interactive team velocity dashboards and automated CSV/PDF export capabilities for sprint accounting and client invoicing.

![Timesheets by AppForest - Time Logging](/projects/timesheets-appforest/time-logging.gif)

## Why it mattered

Published on the Atlassian Marketplace for external customers, the plugin also replaced our legacy time-tracking software internally, saving **$9,000+ in annual licensing costs** and giving leadership real-time visibility into engineering allocations.
