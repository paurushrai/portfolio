At Testlify we were paying for a third-party time-tracking tool that didn't connect to how our engineering work actually happened in Jira. I led the frontend for Timesheets by AppForest, a Jira plugin that handles time tracking, productivity insight, and cost allocation. It's published on the Atlassian Marketplace and open for any team to install — and we run it internally too, where it replaced that paid tool.

![Timesheets by AppForest — Overview](/projects/timesheets-appforest/timesheet.gif)

## What I built

- **Jira integration:** Built natively for the Atlassian Marketplace against Jira's developer APIs, working with the backend engineers to keep OAuth scopes and enterprise security requirements intact.
- **In-Jira UI:** Designed a responsive UI that lives right inside Jira issue panels, with one-click time logging and daily/weekly/monthly views — so engineers log time without switching tools.
- **Reporting:** Built the dashboards that show live productivity data, plus CSV and PDF export so stakeholders can pull reports on demand for cost allocation.

![Timesheets by AppForest — Time Logging](/projects/timesheets-appforest/time-logging.gif)

## Why it mattered

It's a product any team on the Atlassian Marketplace can install. Internally, moving to it let us drop the paid third-party tool, saving over **$9,000 a year**, and gave leadership real-time visibility into team velocity and project cost that helped with forecasting.
