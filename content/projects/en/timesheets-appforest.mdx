While at Testlify, I identified a significant operational bottleneck: the company was spending capital on a disconnected, third-party time-tracking tool that lacked deep integration with our actual engineering workflows. Working alongside a dedicated internal team, I led the frontend engineering for **Timesheets by AppForest**, a custom-built, enterprise-grade Jira plugin designed to seamlessly handle time tracking, real-time productivity insights, and granular cost allocation.

## Technical Scope & Contributions

- **Atlassian Ecosystem Integration:** Navigated complex Jira Developer APIs and UI frameworks to build natively for the Atlassian Marketplace. I collaborated closely with backend engineers to ensure our frontend interfaces properly maintained strict OAuth scopes and respected enterprise-grade security protocols.
- **Frictionless UI/UX within Jira:** Designed a highly responsive UI embedded directly inside Jira issue panels. I engineered features for frictionless "one-click" time logging and custom timeframes (daily, weekly, monthly), removing the context-switching penalty engineers typically face when tracking their hours.
- **Real-Time Data & Reporting Pipelines:** Built comprehensive data-visualization dashboards that process and display live updates on organizational productivity. Furthermore, I engineered robust export pipelines, enabling project stakeholders to instantly generate customizable, on-demand reports in CSV and PDF formats for accurate financial allocation.

## Business Impact

By transitioning the organization to this bespoke internal solution, we completely eliminated our reliance on the expensive third-party SaaS provider, instantly saving the company over **$9,000 annually**. Beyond the immediate cost reduction, the plugin gave leadership unprecedented, real-time visibility into team velocity and project costs, dramatically improving resource forecasting and stakeholder communication across the board.
