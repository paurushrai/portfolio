[GTM Advisors](https://gtmadvisors.com/) wanted a site that matched the caliber of their consulting work and ranked well in search. I owned the build end to end — choosing a Jamstack stack deliberately for the speed and SEO it buys, and setting it up so the marketing team could run the site themselves once I handed it over.

![GTM Advisors](/projects/gtm-advisors/image.png)

## What I built

- **Astro static site:** Astro ships zero JavaScript by default, which kept pages loading fast, scored well on Lighthouse, and gave the site a solid technical footing for SEO.
- **Directus CMS:** Decoupled the content from the frontend with Directus — I set up the schemas and REST APIs so the marketing team could edit content on their own without touching code.
- **Responsive frontend:** Turned the designs into a responsive, component-based frontend that stays maintainable as the site grows.

## Why it mattered

The Jamstack setup cut hosting costs and made content updates self-serve for the marketing team. The fast, SEO-friendly foundation supports their organic search and lead generation.
