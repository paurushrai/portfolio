For [Win Daily Sports](https://windailysports.com/) (in association with Modocosm), I spearheaded the development of advanced sports betting projection models. This project was centered around engineering a fully automated data pipeline to aggregate, process, and analyze massive amounts of sports data.

![Win Daily Sports Dashboard](/projects/win-daily-sports/image.png)

## Key Contributions & Achievements

- **Automated Data Pipelines (ETL):** Designed and deployed robust web scraping scripts to extract real-time and historical sports data from various internet sources and APIs.
- **Predictive Modeling:** Engineered sophisticated projection models designed to analyze player and team statistics, identifying high-value betting opportunities and actionable insights.
- **Workflow Automation:** Streamlined the entire data lifecycle—from extraction to model execution—minimizing manual intervention and ensuring data freshness and accuracy.
- **Google Sheets Integration:** Built and automated the final delivery mechanism, integrating the projection models directly into Google Sheets APIs and AppScripts. This provided stakeholders with an intuitive, dynamic, and easily accessible dashboard for real-time decision-making.

## Business Impact

By automating the data collection and modeling process, I significantly reduced the time-to-insight for the analysts. The projection models empowered the team with data-driven strategies, enhancing the overall quality and accuracy of their betting predictions while scaling their operational capabilities.
